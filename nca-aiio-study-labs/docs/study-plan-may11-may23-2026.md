## Day-by-day details

### May 11

- Read the official certification page and blueprint.
- Start the architecture mapping lab.
- Create a topic tracker with `strong / medium / weak` tags.

### May 12

- Cover AI/ML/DL concept differences.
- Study training vs inference infrastructure needs.
- Write 10 quick recall questions.

### May 13

- Map the NVIDIA software stack at a high level.
- Understand where CUDA, containers, orchestration, and observability tools fit.
- Practice explaining stack layers in 60 seconds.

### May 14

- Focus on hardware requirements for AI workloads.
- Cover compute, memory, storage, and node-level planning.
- Document at least two on-prem vs cloud tradeoffs.

### May 15

- Study networking requirements and DC protocols.
- Review power and cooling constraints for dense GPU clusters.
- Build a quick failure mode table.

### May 16

- Learn cluster component interactions.
- Practice scaling scenarios for different workload mixes.
- Draw cluster topology without notes.

### May 17

- Define operational KPIs (availability, utilization, error rate, latency).
- Draft alert severity levels and escalation path.
- Prepare incident runbook template.

### May 18

- Run telemetry lab script and tune thresholds.
- Identify false positives vs true incidents.
- Link telemetry signals to likely root causes.

### May 19

- Study orchestration and job scheduling essentials.
- Review virtualization considerations in accelerated infrastructure.
- Validate workload placement logic with sample requests.

### May 20

- Complete incident response lab from scenario input.
- Score runbook completeness and sequence quality.
- Update final checklist for exam-day recall.

### May 21

- Take timed mock exam #1 (60 minutes).
- Categorize every miss by domain and reason.
- Repair only the top two weak domains.

### May 22

- Take timed mock exam #2.
- Light review only, no brand-new topics.
- Re-read infographics and diagrams.

### May 23 (Saturday) Exam Day

- 20-minute warm-up only.
- First pass: answer clear items quickly.
- Second pass: return to marked items.

## Time split by blueprint

Allocate your total prep hours close to:

- 40% AI Infrastructure

Because infrastructure is highest-weight and most detailed, prioritize it first during weak-area repair.
