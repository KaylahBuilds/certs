# CNPA Priority Bootcamp (6 Days)

## Goal

Demonstrate platform engineering associate-level competency by defining platform capabilities, developer self-service, guardrails, and measurable outcomes.

## Day Plan

1. Day 1: Platform capability model
- Define what the platform product offers: provisioning, deployment, observability, and policy controls.

2. Day 2: Developer self-service design
- Model intake API/template and golden path workflow.
- Define input validation and ownership model.

3. Day 3: Delivery and environment strategy
- Model GitOps promotion flow across environments.
- Define change control and rollback boundaries.

4. Day 4: Guardrails and compliance
- Add policy controls for approved registries, labels, and baseline security standards.

5. Day 5: Metrics and KPIs
- Define baseline and target values for deployment frequency, lead time, MTTR, and change failure rate.

6. Day 6: Timed design review
- Walk through platform API, templates, controls, and KPI model in one narrative.

## Pass Criteria

- Explain your platform as a product with users, interfaces, and SLAs.
- Show reusable self-service artifacts and policy guardrails.
- Present a measurable platform scorecard.
