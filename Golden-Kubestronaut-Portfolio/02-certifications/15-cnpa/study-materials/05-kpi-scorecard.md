# CNPA KPI Scorecard

## DORA Baseline and Targets

- Deployment Frequency: baseline weekly, target daily
- Lead Time for Changes: baseline 3 days, target < 1 day
- Change Failure Rate: baseline 18 percent, target < 8 percent
- MTTR: baseline 4 hours, target < 1 hour

## Platform Experience KPIs

- Time to first deployment for new service
- Percentage of services onboarded to golden path
- Policy compliance pass rate
- Developer satisfaction score from quarterly survey

## Review Cadence

- Weekly: leading indicators (delivery speed, failure rate)
- Monthly: platform adoption and reliability trends
- Quarterly: roadmap reprioritization based on KPI movement
