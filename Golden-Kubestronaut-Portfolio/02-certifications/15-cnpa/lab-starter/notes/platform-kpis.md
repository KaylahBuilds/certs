# Platform KPI Notes

## Baseline

- Deployment frequency: weekly
- Lead time for changes: 3 days
- Change failure rate: 18 percent
- MTTR: 4 hours

## Target (90-Day Program)

- Deployment frequency: daily
- Lead time for changes: less than 1 day
- Change failure rate: below 8 percent
- MTTR: less than 1 hour

## Adoption Metrics

- Percentage of services using golden template
- Policy pass rate on first deployment
- Time to first deploy for new team onboarding

## Risk Notes

- Tool adoption without platform documentation can reduce KPI improvement.
- Guardrails in audit mode should transition to enforce mode after pilot validation.
