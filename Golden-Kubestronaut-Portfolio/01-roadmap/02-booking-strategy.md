# Exam Booking Strategy (90-Day Mode)

## Rules

- Keep all 16 provisional exam dates blocked in your calendar from Day 1.
- Book exams in rolling sets of 3 so you can shift if one exam slips.
- Use two checkpoints before booking final time slots: mini-mock score and lab reproducibility.

## Booking Gates

- Multiple-choice exam gate: 80% average across 3 timed quizzes.
- Performance exam gate: finish lab from scratch inside target time twice.

## Risk Management

- Keep Day 90 as a hard recovery buffer.
- Overlap prep windows, but do not take two performance exams on consecutive days.
- Reserve your highest-energy slots for CKA, CKS, and CNPE.
- If one performance exam slips, immediately compress one multiple-choice window by 1 day.
